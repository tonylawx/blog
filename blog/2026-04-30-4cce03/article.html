US Stocks · Options · News · Views

0 · Market Assessment

AI capital spending remains the central theme for US equities. Earnings and guidance from GOOG, AMZN, META, and MSFT show that mega-cap technology companies are still committed to heavy AI infrastructure investment in 2026. Short-term volatility does not change that medium-term thesis, but the market is becoming more selective. Companies that can fund investment with strong cash flow are more likely to maintain premium valuations, while those under greater cash-flow pressure will need to prove that the spending produces an acceptable return.

At the index level, risk appetite is still being supported by a relatively small group of heavyweight technology stocks. Semiconductors remain closely tied to CapEx expectations from the cloud hyperscalers. As long as AI orders, infrastructure spending, and compute demand do not materially weaken, GOOG, AMZN, META, MSFT, and the SOXX/SMH complex remain the main vehicles through which investors are pricing the theme.

1 · Risk Watch

The biggest risk is the tension between rising CapEx and weaker free cash flow. Faster AI spending can support revenue expectations and the broader compute cycle, but if cash burn accelerates and payback periods lengthen, investors will shift from rewarding the growth narrative to demanding evidence of profit and cash-flow returns. High-multiple technology stocks would be the first to feel that change.

The second risk is that earnings expectations have already moved higher. Any deterioration in orders, gross margins, cloud growth, depreciation, or regulation could produce a larger market reaction from here. After sharp post-earnings moves, semiconductor ETFs and leading technology names are better approached with discipline than chased at peak sentiment.

2 · Names to Watch

The main focus remains the four cloud and AI leaders: GOOG, AMZN, META, and MSFT. For GOOG and MSFT, the key question is whether cloud growth and their model ecosystems continue to validate the investment cycle. For AMZN, watch AWS growth against the free-cash-flow pressure created by higher CapEx. For META, the central issue is whether its advertising cash engine can continue funding large AI infrastructure commitments without materially weakening shareholder returns.

In semiconductors, the focus remains the AI-server, GPU, ASIC, HBM, and advanced-packaging supply chain, including AMD, SOXX, SMH, and related names. If hyperscalers continue to raise CapEx, the sector retains fundamental support. From a trading perspective, however, investors should distinguish core trend positions from short-term earnings trades and avoid treating strong sentiment as risk-free upside.

3 · Trading Strategy

I would not fade the AI investment cycle without evidence that the fundamentals have changed, but I also would not add aggressively after volatile post-earnings moves. A more disciplined approach is to keep core positions and use pullbacks to key moving averages, retests of prior highs, or post-earnings consolidation to scale into GOOG, AMZN, META, MSFT, and semiconductor ETFs.

Risk control matters more than maximizing a single day’s P&L. If a stock breaks key support on heavy volume or its earnings thesis is undermined by deteriorating cash flow, trim first rather than average down automatically. If the indexes continue to be led higher by the strongest AI names, stay with the leaders while using position limits and predefined risk levels to manage drawdowns.
