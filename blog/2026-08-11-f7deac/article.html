Three drivers: oil, rates, AI

The tape is still organized around oil, rates, and AI.

On oil, the Iran path is unclear, but mutual restraint expectations argue against an overnight sprint above $100 — chop yes, panic spike harder. Energy feeds inflation; capped upside gives the Fed more room to stay on hold.

The risk is data discipline: officials lean “data-dependent” with less forward guidance. One or two hot inflation prints can bring hikes back fast, lift 10Y/20Y/30Y, and compress multiples.

On AI, Q2 for cores and semis was mostly fine. Among the mega-caps, META lagged a bit while others monetized AI more cleanly; 2026 CapEx has not been cut and some raised plans, with AMD and peers lifting outlooks — August feels less stressed than July’s washout. Watch three risks: a geopolitical deal that drags into midterms, hyperscaler CapEx growth slowing (not collapsing), and memory demand cuts from price or financing/credit stress flowing back through the chip chain.

2 · RISK

GOOG: mark-to-market giveback, pullback to load

GOOG fell about 3.61% on a quiet tape with no fresh company headline. The cleaner mechanism is the investment book: a heavy SpaceX stake booked large unrealized gains into Q2 EPS; a SpaceX valuation reset can reverse that mark and pressure Q3 profit optics.

If the fundamental valuation already strips that investment gain, the story does not need a full rewrite. Technically, the post-144 breakout structure is intact — more “accelerating pullback to pick up passengers” than trend failure for underweight holders.

Software: IGV stuck at 107

IGV is capped at 107, failed for two sessions, and closed near 103–104. The hard band is 101–107; a volume break of 107 opens a new up-leg, a rejection is a high-base top risk. Do not force a breakout before it prints.

Names track the industry:

• ORCL: follows IGV, little idiosyncratic drive. Dense supply every ~$10: 209–226, then 232–240; support 147–159.

• PLTR: jammed in 175–192; clear 192 for highs, fail and 134–145 (gap/thrust zone) is in play. Finished swing traders can lighten and reload above 192.

• CRM: near 197 — needs volume through 198; structure resistance 235–277 (~20–30% if the industry cooperates), still gated by IGV 107.

• INTU: bounce-follower. Resistance 346–363; reclaim 426 to confirm trend, else treat as a large rebound. Volume has not dried up fully; some incremental buying shows lower.

• APP: gapped ~6% (near 318) after a downgrade questioning ~30% 2027 growth. Defend 317–350; a close/open under 317 is a risk-off trigger.

• ADBE: ORCL-like structure, slightly better level. Support 235–248; testing 255–268 (200-day). A 200-day reclaim can invite quant bids toward 322–361.

Cyber trio PANW / CRWD / FTNT coil near highs — rich, but the breakout structures are not broken yet.
