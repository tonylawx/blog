The Selloff Reaches the Next Support Range

On November 20, the S&P 500 closed at 6,910.39, down 3.70% on the day and 8.61% for the week. The Nasdaq Composite lost 5.28%, the Nasdaq 100 fell 5.61%, the Dow declined 1.93%, and the Russell 2000 fell 2.76%. The VIX rose to 40.03 during the session before finishing at 35.89. The S&P 500 has now fallen for seven straight sessions, its longest streak since February 2020. A constituent snapshot showed 376 decliners, fewer than Thursday's 464. Consumer staples was the only sector to finish higher.

Nvidia Faces Questions While the Shares Break Lower

NVDA closed at $125.53, down 14.01% on Friday and 22.92% on the week, and was below the $132 reference level. Reuters reported at approximately 2:13 p.m. Eastern that Securities and Exchange Commission enforcement staff were examining potential accounting issues, citing two people briefed on the matter. The agency declined to comment, and Nvidia had not immediately responded to a request for comment at the time of the report.

The scope of the reported inquiry was not disclosed. An inquiry is not a finding of wrongdoing. Nvidia shares had already fallen more than 6% before the Reuters report, so it would be inaccurate to attribute the entire day's decline to that report. Investors still need to distinguish confirmed company disclosures from allegations and establish which questions investigators are actually examining.

Separately, Nikkei reported that at least two Nvidia server partners had suspended shipments to customers in Southeast Asia amid changing U.S. export controls. This is a media report about partners' actions, not a company announcement that all shipments have stopped. The size, duration and financial impact remain uncertain.

The price break and the reports raise separate questions. A short-term rebound would not by itself resolve an inquiry or restore the prior chart structure. Conversely, an investigation report alone does not establish how much future revenue will be affected. The next pieces of evidence are the companies' disclosures and whether buyers can defend a new range.

Apple Is Exploring Agents as AI Spending Faces Scrutiny

Bloomberg reported on November 19 that Apple was developing an AI agent capable of helping users operate devices, use apps, browse and perform other tasks. Apple job listings provide evidence of the work. They do not establish a product launch date, the final features or commercial success. Google is also investing in AI tools, and competition is increasing across consumer and enterprise software.

Apple's capital spending offers a useful comparison if the accounting is kept clear. Its fiscal 2026 cash payments for property, plant and equipment were $9.041 billion, compared with $12.715 billion in fiscal 2025. That is companywide capital spending, not an AI-only budget. Research and development expense was about $45.8 billion, and Apple also uses outside cloud services and leases infrastructure. Lower reported capital spending does not mean its AI work costs nothing.

Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo, citing supply-chain research, estimated that planned 2027 shipments of Google TPU 8i chips designed with Broadcom could be reduced by roughly one million units, or about 20%. Google and Broadcom have not confirmed the estimate. It concerns that chip generation, not all TPU orders or total AI capital spending.

A separate supply-chain report said Google sought packaging support from TSMC after testing Intel's technology. Order volumes and any production transfer await confirmation.

The investment question is who can turn useful products into revenue without letting computing, infrastructure and distribution costs rise faster than returns. A product announcement, hiring and actual customer adoption each answer a different part of that question. The market is still waiting for more evidence about how those economics develop.

Positioning Can Add Volatility in Both Directions

Trend-following funds and options dealers can influence the speed of a move, but models describe scenarios rather than confirmed flows. 7,084 on the S&P 500 is a reference in one CTA model. Whether a fund buys or sells depends on its positions, rules and subsequent prices. It is not evidence that buying will begin at an exact index level.

November 20 was also a monthly options-expiration session. Expiring positions can change hedging needs, yet expiration alone does not prove a record gamma exposure, identify the source of every trade or establish that forced selling is over. A move above a model level is more useful when accompanied by continued demand and broader participation.

The coming holiday changes the calendar. U.S. equity markets have regular sessions Monday through Wednesday, are closed Thursday, November 26 for Thanksgiving, and close at 1 p.m. Eastern on Friday. Shorter sessions and thinner participation can make an individual move harder to interpret.

TSMC Breaks Below Its Prior Range

TSM closed at $318.71, down 9.48%. It moved below the previously discussed $320 to $350 range. A range that had held as a reference is less informative once the stock trades through it; the next observation is whether selling slows and the price can stabilize or reclaim part of that area.

A market commentator described waiting for stabilization before considering a position. That is his conditional approach, not a verified trade and not a statement about the reader's holdings. Buying simply because a stock has entered a previously identified range can be risky when the price continues through it.

Microsoft, Google, Tesla, Intuitive Surgical, Netflix and Travelers bring different business exposures to the same market. Their results and financing risks should be examined separately rather than assuming that one price move explains every company. Cash flow, operating performance and the ability to fund investment remain useful checks alongside technical levels.

For the next session, the immediate test is SPY's response inside its current range. A hold could give buyers time to build a rebound. A confirmed move below the lower edge would shift attention to the next downside area. Neither outcome is guaranteed, and the holiday calendar makes follow-through especially important.
