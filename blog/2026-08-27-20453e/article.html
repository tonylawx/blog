The Index Rallied; Most Stocks Did Not

Thursday, August 27, the cash session settled Wednesday night’s prints. The Dow gained 0.2% to 53,569. SPX rose 0.7% to 7,731. Nasdaq rose 1.6% to 26,541. SOX gained 2.33% to 11,882. SOXX closed 525.43, up 1.95%.

The averages were green. The vote was not. A majority of SPX names fell. NVDA more than offset them. Information technology was one of the few groups that actually led. The 10-year yield finished around 4.672%, barely a basis point higher. The 30-year was about 5.19%, the 2-year about 4.23%. Bonds did not want to pick a side before Friday’s speech. Initial jobless claims were 203,000, under about 208,000. Continuing claims were about 1.78 million. Macro did not get in the way and did not open a second story. Friday is Kevin Warsh’s first Jackson Hole as Chair. The market wants inflation, the long end, and whether Treasury buybacks of long bonds count as policy. Rates stay in a wait-and-see book.

The real pricing sat in two names. NVDA closed 227.98, up 8.74%, after a high of 230.47. The last four quarters, a beat still sold the next day. That habit broke. CRM ran from 205.62 to 252.05, up 22.58%, high 254.48. Wednesday after hours gave about 12%. Thursday cash added another leg. The index is up. Most stocks are split. A handful of leaders are carrying the average. How far this green bar goes depends on whether 236 and 277 can actually clear.

1 · SOFTWARE

Salesforce Took 235; Software Followed a Slice

Wednesday after hours CRM stalled near 235. That was the heavy shelf. Thursday it opened 230.05 — also the low — and ran to 254. 235 flipped from resistance to the day’s floor. 277 is still above. So is the 52-week high at 269. After hours the question was a fade. Cash answered first: get through the shelf.

Second-quarter adjusted EPS was $5.90 on $11.35 billion of revenue. About $2.53 of that $5.90 is strategic-investment gains, on the order of $2.6 billion, largely an Anthropic stake. Operating profit is not as explosive as the headline. What broke the price was the guide and the interface: fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS $16.67 to $16.71, revenue $46.1 to $46.4 billion, and Claudeforce wiring the customer system into Claude. The prior story was that AI agents would replace high-margin subscriptions. Thursday’s tape wrote the other side first: be the pipe, not the name that gets replaced.

Software followed. ADBE closed 289.15, up 5.73%. CRWD closed 227.96, about 20% above Wednesday’s 189, high 229, open 208. In cybersecurity PANW tagged along about 13%. Others ran harder. Tuesday’s INTU cut price to defend share — defense. Thursday’s CRM was offense. Same software line, two days, two prices.

Clearing 235 is not owning 277. Long-term holders can give it time. Near term, watch whether 235 becomes support on a retest. If it holds, 277 is the next map. If it fails, 252 is premium. Buyers who took 230 at the open already treated Wednesday’s cap as Thursday’s floor. Whether that floor is still there tomorrow matters more than another 10 points up.

2 · FOCUS

Nvidia Paid a Cash Premium; The High Is Still 236

NVDA opened 222.86, low 220.90, high 230.47, close 227.98. Wednesday cash was 209.66. After hours it dumped then recovered toward 218. Thursday never tagged 218. The book was made above 220. Market cap added about $440 billion in a day. Four quarters of “beat then fade” broke. The old high did not.

236 has been the wait. Recent tests tagged 227 and 230 and failed. Thursday’s high was 230.47 — at the door again. 218 is no longer the question. 236 still is. What cash bought from the call is this line: fiscal 2028 revenue growth about 70%, and management said demand is larger than 70% with supply the constraint. Third-quarter guide is $108 billion plus or minus 2%, gross margin 73.5% to 74.5%. Some buyers had $109 billion. Margin gave a point. Cash bought demand first and left margin for later.

Chips followed, but not together. AVGO closed 371.54, up 4.49%. SOX added 2.33%. MU closed 935.39, down 0.32%, after gapping toward 967 and giving it back. Memory was written Wednesday as a beneficiary of that 70% fiscal 2028 growth. Thursday did not keep the premium. The demand story traded in NVDA and AVGO. Memory got blocked by valuation and noise. Circular credit, receivables, and guarantees were already on the table Wednesday. Thursday did not price them as a smash, and did not clear them. The demand story can still be told. The cash-conversion ledger is still behind it.

Losing 218 is what would invalidate Thursday’s bar. That has not happened. Adding size wants 236 taken and held. Calling a new high at 230 is early. The cap is already near $5 trillion. The bid has to be real. The window opened. The high is still up there. The four-quarter fade habit was interrupted for a day. An interruption is not confirmation. Confirmation is 236, not 227.

3 · FOCUS

Meta Did Not Open the Box; Friday Hands It to Warsh

Wednesday’s META settlement did not become a Thursday rally. It closed 571.10, down 0.87%, high 588.39, low 567.62. 588 tagged the lower half of the 581 to 632 box, then slipped back under 581. $18 billion over ten years, with 30% still tied to TikTok and YouTube matching the limits, took noise off. The slower AI-monetization path is not a one-day legal fix. It looks like the cheapest of the four megacaps. Thursday still did not buy it as a leader. 581 to 632 remains a box. Closing under the floor looks even less like a break.

The averages can print green on NVDA and CRM, names that already delivered numbers. Most stocks did not vote with them. That structure does not love a sudden tightening in rate expectations. If Warsh stays data-dependent and does not lock September, this bar can still be used. If he talks the long end and inflation hard, the bid leaves the follower software and chips first.

The tree is clear. Bullish: NVDA holds the 227 area and actually prints 236, CRM turns 235 into support and tries 277, software and chips follow, and Warsh does not tighten the path. Neutral: 227 to 230 digests, 236 stays a same-day ceiling, CRM chops 235 to 255, the index is still a handful of leaders, and most names lag. Bearish: 218 is given back, CRM loses 235, names like MU that did not follow crack first, Warsh hardens September, and Thursday’s bar is a pass-through.

The window is Friday’s speech, then whether 236 clears. Revenue and guide already got a cash-session grade. The new high has not.
