0 · MARKET

The indexes barely moved

The Dow closed at 51,850.55, up 0.38%. The S&P 500 closed at 7,807.24, down 0.06%. The Nasdaq composite closed at 27,245.90, down 0.44%. The Dow rose. The Nasdaq paid for semiconductors. The S&P sat between them, a few dollars from 7,800.

Four points off the S&P is not a risk-on signal, and it is not a breakdown. The index did not discover a new price. The Philadelphia semiconductor index did. It fell 2.67% to 12,236.79.

Memory priced a rival that has not shipped

On October 10, CXMT president Cao Kanyu said the company plans DDR5 server memory on a 4F² cell by year-end 2026. The South China Morning Post and TechNode both carried the plan. Neither one had volume yields or a server qualification.

Monday, memory sold off anyway. MU closed at 989.04, down 3.88%, back under 1,000. SNDK closed at 1,487.33, down 5.97%. Memory fell harder than the semiconductor index, and the semiconductor index fell harder than the Nasdaq.

Shipments did not change on Monday. Traders marked down supply that might show up later. If MU fails to reclaim 1,000, the pullback is a break. A close back through 1,000 means Monday was a discount on a plan, not a new regime.

SNDK fell more than MU. NAND and DRAM do not share a production line. They were sold in the same basket. The deeper NAND print does not close the memory cycle. Yields and customer qualification do.

2 · FOCUS

The megacaps split

META closed at 742.96, up 3.38%. MSFT closed at 541.15, up 1.14%. NVDA fell 1.29%. Platforms gained. Chips and memory fell. Most of the Nasdaq's 0.44% drop came from chips and memory.

A 1.29% decline in NVDA is not a long-term reset. The group fell more than two percent, and the stocks followed the group. Adding chips while the group is still bleeding is early. META and MSFT rising does not mean the index is breaking either. That takes the S&P leaving 7,800.

3 · FOCUS

7,800 is still the line

The S&P closed at 7,807 and never left 7,800. It also never tested last week's highs. The next round number on the Nasdaq is 27,000. That level was not touched. MU at 1,000 is the level that was.

Inflation prints are still ahead. Monday's flat index is not a new trend. Memory has to retake 1,000, or the S&P has to walk away from 7,800. Until one of those happens, the flat index is just a flat index.
