The Board Reinstates Nvidia's Founder

Nvidia announced on November 23 that Jensen Huang was back as president, CEO and a director. Its SEC filing says the board reinstated him effective November 22. Nvidia's board said it concluded after consulting senior U.S. officials that his return presented no national security risk. An independent board committee's investigation continues, and a separate committee oversees national security and export controls. California's attorney general said the state inquiry would continue.

NVDA gained 31.48% to close at $165.05 after trading as high as $171.50. That gets the stock back inside the broad $132–$194 range. Watch $150 if sellers return. On the upside, $174 is the first test and $194 is the bigger hurdle. For the board, the next test is how it applies the new oversight to funding, guarantees and contract commitments. My subjective view is that $150 is the first support test, $174 the first upside checkpoint and $194 the larger hurdle. The leadership change may reduce one source of uncertainty, but the ongoing investigation and export-control oversight mean the risk is not gone.

The Short Week Brings a Test for the S&P 500

Nasdaq futures reached their 7% premarket upside limit. The Nasdaq Composite ended up 6.51%, and SOXX gained 13.63%. The S&P 500 rose 4.73% to 7,237.17 after trading between 7,152.66 and 7,295.60. The VIX fell from 35.89 to 26.62. That is a sharp drop in the volatility priced by S&P 500 options, with the reading still above its midmonth levels. Short covering and rule-based rebalancing can reinforce a rally this fast. The issuer of the three-times inverse Nvidia product NV3S confirmed an intraday rebalance. ETF Stream reported that the product fell about 91.4% before a brief trading pause and later reopened.

Watch how the S&P trades around 7,200. If it loses that area, 7,100 comes into view. If it reaches 7,300–7,400, watch whether the advance spreads beyond the initial leaders. SPY rose 4.71% to close at $722.29, below the open of $724.33. For the ETF, $728–$740 is the first area to reclaim. A close above $753 would strengthen the case for a wider reversal, while $671 is the lower reference in a deeper selloff. U.S. stock exchanges are closed Thursday for Thanksgiving and close early on Friday, November 27, at 1 p.m. Eastern time.

Taiwan Semiconductor Gives Back Part of the Opening Gap

TSM opened at $385.49 and closed at $363.60, up 14.08% from Friday's $318.71 close. Its Monday low of $361.66 was above Friday's $343.75 high. Friday's high was itself below Thursday's $351.136 low. Friday sits between two gaps on the daily chart, an island reversal pattern. Monday's close well below the open puts the focus on what buyers do next.

My subjective view is that a TSM entry should depend on price confirmation, not another investor's reported position. The $320–$350 area is one possible watch zone if a daily reversal forms; a sustained, high-volume breakout is another. Without either confirmation, there is no need to force an entry.
