Anthropic and changing expectations

Some investors are discussing an Anthropic valuation near one point five trillion dollars for a possible initial public offering. That is below earlier expectations of around two trillion dollars.

Anthropic reported third quarter revenue of sixteen point eight billion dollars, up forty five percent from the prior quarter. The company is still growing rapidly. But its growth rate has slowed, and investors are now asking how much they should pay for future growth.

The question is not simply whether artificial intelligence will be used. It is how much infrastructure must be financed, how long it takes to earn that money back, and how much cash remains after interest expense. Recent pressure on artificial intelligence infrastructure stocks and debt shows that funding has become a risk to watch.

Taiwan Semiconductor has strong numbers, but price still matters

Taiwan Semiconductor reported third quarter revenue of about one point four nine trillion New Taiwan dollars. In American dollars, that is forty six point seven two billion, up about fifty one percent from a year ago. Net income rose nearly sixty nine percent. Earnings per share were twenty nine point four three New Taiwan dollars, or four dollars and sixty cents per American depositary receipt.

Fourth quarter revenue guidance is fifty two point three to fifty four point three billion dollars. Gross margin guidance is sixty five to sixty seven percent. Those are strong numbers. Still, third quarter gross margin came in at sixty six point four percent, a little below the roughly sixty six point five percent expectation mentioned in the market discussion. The early ramp of two nanometer production could put some pressure on margins for a while.

The price plan for T S M is conditional. Four hundred ten to four hundred twenty four dollars is aggressive. Four hundred to four hundred six is a possible area for staged buying. Three hundred sixty to three hundred seventy eight is more attractive for adding. Three hundred twenty to three hundred fifty would be a deeper panic zone.

Around four hundred twelve dollars, the stock is not obviously cheap. Existing holders with a low cost basis can wait. Investors without a position do not have to chase it.

4 · MARKET

Keep room to act next week

There are three kinds of positions to separate. First, durable businesses that may become more attractive at lower prices. Second, holdings that have broken support and need risk control. Third, high volatility names that still do not offer a clear entry.

Cash gives you flexibility. Buying too early means sitting through a drawdown. Waiting means preserving the ability to act when the risk reward improves.

Next week, watch whether the major indexes lose support, whether semiconductor selling continues, and whether any rebound can hold. Artificial intelligence demand can remain real while the market pays less for future growth. Both things can be true at once.
