A Strong Rebound That Left Nvidia Behind

On Monday, November ninth, twenty twenty six, American stocks rallied sharply. The S and P five hundred gained about two point four percent. The Nasdaq Composite rose nearly two point seven percent. The Dow added just over two percent.

The Nasdaq one hundred rose about three point four percent. Brent crude prices fell nearly six percent. The Vix volatility index dropped to roughly twenty. But Nvidia did not join the rally. Nvidia fell more than four percent and finished at one hundred and seventy eight dollars and sixty seven cents.

A M D rose more than thirteen percent. Broadcom gained almost nine percent. That tells us the market was not rejecting every artificial intelligence stock. Investors were reacting to company specific risks.

1 · MARKET

Nvidia's Chief Executive Is Under Formal Scrutiny

On November eighth, Nvidia released a message from its chief executive, Jensen Huang. He acknowledged that he had been unfaithful to his wife and apologized. The company's board also announced an independent investigation. The investigation concerns reports that Huang may have been targeted by a foreign intelligence operation.

The company has not announced a finding that secrets were stolen or that export laws were broken. In fact, its board said it had no reason to believe that national security or company information had been compromised. The board also expressed confidence in Huang's leadership.

After Monday's stock market close, shareholders filed a proposed class action. It alleges that investors were not properly informed about relevant risks. A lawsuit is not a court finding. And an after hours filing could not have caused the entire decline earlier that day.

There are three questions for investors. First, will the investigation create a lasting governance discount? Second, will new evidence lead to regulatory problems or affect customer relationships? Third, could the board's support for Huang change? None of those outcomes is confirmed today.

Monday's price action is important. Nvidia opened close to one hundred and seventy three dollars. It traded down to about one hundred and seventy dollars and twenty four cents. Then it recovered to finish above one hundred and seventy eight dollars.

So the stock broke below one hundred and seventy four dollars during the day, but not on a closing basis. A trader using an intraday stop could have sold. Someone waiting for a closing break may have held. The next price references are the low near one hundred and seventy, and the recovery area from one hundred and seventy four to one hundred and ninety four.

If the shares fail again, the one hundred and fifty to one hundred and sixty dollar area may become relevant. That is a risk scenario, not a prediction. Nvidia will report third quarter fiscal twenty twenty seven results on November seventeenth.

The market will have to consider the earnings outlook and the leadership investigation together.

2 · MARKET

A M D Has Reasons to Rise Beyond Nvidia's Problems

A M D jumped more than thirteen percent to roughly five hundred and nine dollars. The company announced that its E P Y C processors will support Google Cloud's new T P U system. These are host processors working alongside Google's accelerators. They do not replace the Google T P U chips.

Oppenheimer also upgraded the stock and set a six hundred dollar price target. That gives A M D company specific catalysts, beyond a shift away from Nvidia. The risk is cash conversion. A M D generated about five hundred and fifty four million dollars of quarterly free cash flow.

That was down from more than one and a half billion in the preceding quarter. Investors should look for stronger collections, not just higher revenue. For A M D, a sustained move through the five hundred and one to five hundred and twenty eight dollar range would help confirm momentum.

Taiwan Semiconductor also recovered toward three hundred and ninety dollars. A move beyond four hundred and twenty four dollars would be a stronger trend signal. A decline toward three hundred and sixty to three hundred and seventy eight could offer another area to reassess the business and price action.

These are watch levels, not guaranteed entry prices.

3 · MARKET

Systematic Trading Can Amplify Both Directions

The S and P five hundred finished Monday near seven thousand five hundred and fifty. Some systematic trading models discuss seven thousand six hundred and seventy six as an upper trigger. They also highlight seven thousand and eighty four as a deeper downside reference.

These are model scenarios, not guaranteed buying or selling orders. Such estimates can change rapidly with volatility and positioning. Monday's rebound may have involved short covering. But one session is not enough to separate short covering from genuine fresh demand.

Watch whether the major indexes hold their recovery and whether more stocks keep participating. For the Q Q Q fund, the area near six hundred and eighty seven dollars remains a reference. The higher region around seven hundred and twenty eight would require a much stronger advance.

The S O X X semiconductor fund has also completed a three for one stock split. Prices from before and after the split must be adjusted before drawing conclusions.

4 · CAPEX

The Next Test Is CoreWeave's Cash Flow

CoreWeave reports on Tuesday, November tenth. Its quarterly earnings call is scheduled for five o'clock Eastern. Investors will watch capital spending, financing costs, delivery schedules and cash generated from customer contracts. Heavy borrowing can make delayed data centre projects expensive.

Anthropic is also preparing for a possible public offering. But reports say some early pricing details were withdrawn as inaccurate. There is no confirmed final offering price in those reports. October consumer inflation data is due on Tuesday as well.

Nvidia's own earnings follow on November seventeenth. The best outcome would combine calmer inflation, stronger A I cash generation and a clearer picture of Nvidia's governance risks. For a technology heavy portfolio, review position sizes and the cash needed if short puts are assigned.

Protective puts and collars can reduce some risk, but they come with costs. One strong rally does not guarantee that the correction is over.
