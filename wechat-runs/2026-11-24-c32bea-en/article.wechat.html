Jensen Huang Returns to Nvidia

Nvidia announced on November twenty third that Jensen Huang was back as president, chief executive and a director. Its filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission says the board's decision took effect November twenty second. Nvidia's board said it reached its own conclusion after consulting senior United States officials. The board concluded that his return presented no national security risk. An independent board committee continues its investigation. Another committee oversees national security and export controls. California's attorney general says the state inquiry will continue.

Nvidia climbed thirty one point four eight percent and closed at one hundred sixty five dollars and five cents. It traded as high as one hundred seventy one dollars and fifty cents. Shares are back inside a broad range from one hundred thirty two to one hundred ninety four dollars.

Watch one hundred fifty dollars if sellers come back. On the upside, one hundred seventy four is the first test, and one hundred ninety four is a bigger hurdle. For the board, the next test is how those oversight measures work in practice. My view is that one hundred fifty dollars is the first support test. One hundred seventy four dollars is the first upside checkpoint, while one hundred ninety four dollars is a larger hurdle. The leadership change helps, but the investigation and export-control oversight still matter.
