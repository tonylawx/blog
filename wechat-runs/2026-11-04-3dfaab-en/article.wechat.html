A Rising Stock Market Left Semiconductors Behind

On Wednesday, November fourth, twenty twenty six, the Dow jumped three percent. The S and P five hundred gained about one point six percent. The Nasdaq Composite rose about one point four percent. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index went the other way. It fell about three point one percent. Micron, Marvell and Sandisk each lost at least seven percent. Nvidia and Broadcom also finished lower.

Meanwhile, Microsoft jumped roughly six point seven percent. Amazon gained six point one percent, and Alphabet rose just over three percent. This was not a broad collapse in technology. Investors were treating different parts of the artificial intelligence industry very differently.

Lower oil prices and relief after the elections helped the wider market. But the split between cloud companies and chip suppliers deserves closer attention.

1 · MARKET

Elections Raise New Questions About Data Centre Growth

Democrats won control of both houses of Congress in Tuesday's midterm elections. That could increase investigations and hearings involving large technology companies, electricity bills and data centre financing. A new law still faces the president's veto. Congressional control alone does not stop construction.

Texas may be more important for near term approvals. Gina Hinojosa defeated the sitting Republican governor. She will be the state's first Democratic governor in more than thirty years. She has proposed a pause on new data centres. But she has not taken office yet. The details of any future order, and its effect on existing permits, still matter.

If new facilities are delayed, chip and networking equipment deliveries could also move back. Customers may need the computing power, but their purchases and payments may arrive later than expected. That is difficult for operators financing new campuses with borrowed money. Interest bills do not disappear when a permit is delayed.

Existing cloud networks may benefit. Microsoft, Amazon and Google already have customers and usable infrastructure. If demand stays strong while new supply becomes harder to build, those businesses could earn more from their existing capacity. That is a possible explanation for their rally. It is not proof that cloud prices have already gone up. The same permitting restrictions could also limit the cloud giants' future expansion.

2 · RISK

Nvidia and T S M C Need Clear Risk Levels

Nvidia remains an important company for investors with concentrated artificial intelligence exposure. One technical plan uses the range between one hundred and seventy four and one hundred and ninety four dollars as an observation zone. If the shares break and stay below one hundred and seventy four dollars, an investor with a ten percent portfolio weight might cut that exposure to five percent.

A later recovery toward one hundred and fifty to one hundred and sixty dollars would still need evidence of stabilization. These numbers are planning references, not guaranteed price floors. Taiwan Semiconductor has strengths in leading edge manufacturing and long standing customer relationships. Yet its shares can still fall when the broader chip sector is repriced.

One possible first entry zone is three hundred and sixty to three hundred and seventy eight dollars. A second, deeper zone is three hundred and twenty to three hundred and fifty dollars. A lower price is not enough by itself. Look for better price action and more stable earnings expectations.

There is also a share split to remember. The S O X X semiconductor fund completed a three for one stock split after Wednesday's close. From Thursday onward, a lower share price does not represent a sudden loss in value.

3 · RISK

Microsoft and Amazon Are Strong, but Chasing Is Risky

Microsoft and Amazon each jumped more than six percent on Wednesday. They have established cloud businesses and customers who already use their computing capacity. For Microsoft, a previous pullback area near five hundred and thirty four dollars is now well below the latest rally. Six hundred dollars and six hundred and twenty three dollars are scenario targets, not guaranteed outcomes.

If the stock loses the breakout and falls back under five hundred and thirty four dollars, the trend deserves another look. A big one day gain is not automatically a good entry point. Amazon has a broad projected valuation range between two hundred and fifty and three hundred and sixty dollars. That range depends on future earnings assumptions.

A pullback that holds the breakout area may offer a more controlled entry than chasing another gap higher. Google's longer term scenario range sits between two hundred and ninety nine and four hundred and forty nine dollars. Investors can also watch two hundred and seventy dollars as a possible lower entry reference, and two hundred and ninety eight dollars as an area where sellers may appear.

Meta has a suggested longer term staging zone between five hundred and eighty and six hundred and thirty dollars. None of these ranges replaces a fresh earnings forecast.

4 · CONSUMER

The Rotation Also Reaches Healthcare and Consumer Stocks

Intuitive Surgical follows a different business cycle. Its revenue depends on surgical procedures, robot installations and recurring instruments. The area between four hundred and seventy and five hundred dollars is a potential trading ceiling. A sustained breakout above five hundred would strengthen the trend. Repeated failures or a loss of four hundred and fifty eight would call for another risk review.

Investors also bought consumer and healthcare companies after the election. Some expect changes to public spending and assistance programmes. But market expectations are not yet company earnings. Defence stocks face a different risk from budget negotiations. Over the next few weeks, watch whether cloud leaders stay strong and semiconductor stocks begin to recover as well.

If both groups improve, the market could be building a healthier advance. If chip stocks keep falling while only a few cloud companies rise, the rally may stay narrow. For a technology heavy portfolio, check how much is tied to the same investment cycle. Also check whether short put options might require cash for share assignments.

A smaller position or defined risk hedge can reduce exposure. But insurance has a cost. A rising index does not guarantee that every holding is healthy. Companies already collecting cash from their infrastructure have a different risk profile from companies still waiting to build it.
