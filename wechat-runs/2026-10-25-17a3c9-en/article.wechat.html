A Major Valuation Reset as Markets Scrutinize Spending

OpenAI is in active talks for a new funding round of roughly thirty billion dollars. The target valuation was lowered from one point four trillion dollars to one point two five trillion dollars. That is an eleven percent reduction.

This target cut marks a rare voluntary retreat for the leading artificial intelligence company. Behind the headlines, inference costs have fallen by nearly ninety percent over the past year. Open source models have closed the gap with proprietary systems. That pressure triggered price wars that squeezed gross margins on A P I calls.

At the same time, maintaining models and global servers requires massive capital spending. Cloud providers continue to spend tens of billions of dollars each quarter. Investors are no longer paying for model size alone. They are judging artificial intelligence companies by actual cash flow.

Without high margin enterprise revenue, heavy compute investments quickly weigh down corporate balance sheets.

1 · COMPUTE

Amazon Halts Data Center Project as Power Grids Reach Limits

Amazon reached an agreement with Loudoun County in Virginia. Amazon decided to abandon its data center plan on the former George Washington University campus. The one hundred twenty two acre property will now become residential and mixed use housing. Amazon agreed to donate twenty five acres for community space and five million dollars to the local housing fund.

Loudoun County handles roughly thirty percent of global internet traffic. This cancellation exposes the main physical barrier for artificial intelligence infrastructure. That barrier is electrical grid capacity. A cluster of one hundred thousand graphics chips needs hundreds of megawatts of continuous electricity. Local substations and power lines need three to five years to permit and build.

Compute demand is growing exponentially, but power grid expansion remains slow. In chip manufacturing, Taiwan Semiconductor continues to run its three nanometer and two nanometer lines at full capacity. Nvidia resolved packaging yield issues with its Blackwell chips. Even so, server rack deliveries remain delayed by cooling distribution units and power equipment.

Profits remain concentrated with chip designers and foundries. Cloud providers are left absorbing heavy depreciation expenses.

2 · COMPUTE

Microsoft and Google Buy Nuclear Power for Data Centers

Microsoft agreed to buy power from the reopened Three Mile Island nuclear plant. The twenty year contract will provide eight hundred thirty five megawatts of dedicated baseload power. Around the same time, Google signed a deal with Kairos Power. Google committed to buy up to five hundred megawatts of power from small modular reactors.

Major tech companies are building dedicated nuclear power feeds for two clear reasons. First, training runs need steady power. Voltage drops during distributed training can cost millions of dollars in restarted checkpoints. Second, data centers face zero carbon mandates. Cloud providers need round the clock clean energy that wind and solar cannot deliver alone.

This transition marks a major shift. The artificial intelligence race is expanding from software algorithms into heavy industrial competition for power and land.

3 · ENERGY

Capital Shifts Toward Energy Infrastructure and Vertical Workflows

Connecting these three events shows how artificial intelligence is shifting from market hype to operational reality. First, simple wrapper applications and A P I resellers will face an industry shakeout over the next two to three quarters. Falling model prices and built in features from major cloud providers are squeezing software margins. Sustainable revenue will belong only to specialized agent platforms that integrate deeply into company workflows and protect private data.

Second, investor interest is moving from chip designers toward power grids, heavy transformers, and nuclear energy. Nvidia's shipment pace next year will depend heavily on substation upgrades and utility connections. Companies that supply electrical equipment and clean baseload power are drawing long term capital.

The valuation frenzy has cooled down, and the industry is now entering a phase of disciplined execution.
