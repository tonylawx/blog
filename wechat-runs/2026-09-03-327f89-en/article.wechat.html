Waller Turns Dovish as Services Inflation Simmers

On Thursday, September 3, hawkish Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller struck a noticeably dovish tone. Treasury yields moved lower and fueled a broad rally across all four major US equity benchmarks.

The S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Dow Jones Industrial Average all gained more than 1%. Inside the S&P 500, 341 stocks advanced and 160 declined, with 8 out of 11 sectors finishing higher.

In contrast, the semiconductor ETF SOXX lagged, finishing up just 0.1% as constituent stocks split evenly. Capital is rotating away from crowded chip exposure and back into mega-cap platforms and software.

Waller stated that he leans toward keeping rates unchanged at the September meeting rather than hiking. He noted that waiting one meeting carries minimal economic cost, and a three-month inflation pace of 2.8% remains acceptable.

Following his remarks, fed funds futures pared September rate-hike odds to 50%. Meanwhile, August Challenger layoffs rose to 52,881, led by consumer goods companies like Procter & Gamble and Estee Lauder.

Tech layoffs fell to 6,103, and corporate restructuring replaced AI as the primary reason for job cuts for the first time since February.

The August ISM Services PMI rose to 55.4, new orders jumped to 60.9, and the prices paid gauge climbed to 72.3. Services inflation shows little sign of cooling, leaving Friday nonfarm payrolls as the decisive macro catalyst.

Tesla Breaks Out with $331 Defining Swing Structure

Tesla extended its powerful rally that began in late July. Cybercab driverless robotaxis officially entered commercial service in Austin, Texas, sending shares past $368 on heavy volume.

With $368 cleared, Tesla has transitioned into a wider trading range between $315 and $465. A continuation above $465 would directly challenge all-time highs, while losing $315 would return shares to the prior $235 to $326 consolidation zone.

The $315 to $368 region subdivides into a key pivot between $331 and $368. A shallow pullback that holds above $331 would represent constructive digestion, offering an attractive tactical entry for swing traders.

A break below $331 would shift the center of gravity downward, significantly raising the odds of testing the lower bound at $315.

Weekly and monthly charts remain firmly bullish. However, the daily chart retains an unreset bearish momentum divergence dating back to August 21.

Given elevated volatility, Tesla warrants disciplined swing execution around defined risk levels rather than long-term position building at current prices.

Microsoft Overhauls Segments to Showcase AI Monetization

Microsoft advanced 2% on Thursday, fully erasing three sessions of declines and reclaiming near-term overhead resistance.

Microsoft announced a comprehensive restructuring of its financial reporting beginning in the first quarter of fiscal 2027. Its three legacy operating segments will be consolidated into two divisions: Agent and Infrastructure, and Devices and Consumers.

This reorganization merges commercial cloud and enterprise software into a single unified AI infrastructure stack while isolating consumer hardware into a separate unit.

When enterprise AI monetization lags expectations, management typically obscures performance inside broader conglomerates. By proactively breaking out AI-driven commercial operations, Microsoft signals high conviction in its AI revenue run rate.

Transparently exposing this foundation should also alleviate concerns over rising credit default swap spreads tied to free cash flow consumption.

Microsoft rebounded sharply after testing a low of $496 on Wednesday. The critical gating level remains $514, where a valid breakout requires an open or close above that mark.

As a core portfolio anchor, short-term chop requires no reaction, with the long-term valuation objective pegged above $600.

Nvidia Sets Fresh Highs as AMD Follows the Script

Nvidia continued to ride momentum from the G20 infrastructure remarks, closing at a fresh post-earnings high of $229. Daily, weekly, and monthly moving averages remain in textbook bullish alignment.

Following more than a year of consolidation and a firm base built since April 2026, valuation at $230 remains compelling. Scale dampens high-beta velocity but offers exceptional defensive resilience, justifying an unchanged 10% core allocation.

Within semiconductors, investor attention is shifting toward AMD. Trading at $456, AMD is mirroring the base-building pattern previously mapped out by Nvidia.

Based on 6 to 12-month forward consensus for fiscal 2027, $456 does not look stretched.

Overhead resistance spans $467 to $498. A decisive breakout above $498 would unleash accumulated momentum toward $506 to $528, with a clean move above $528 clearing the path toward record territory.

On the downside, the key line in the sand sits at $427. Losing $427 would leave support thin until $366, making the resolution between $427 and $498 decisive for the intermediate trend.
