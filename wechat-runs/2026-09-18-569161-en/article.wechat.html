Indexes mixed. Treat it as the witching close first

The Friday tape did not give a direction.

SPX closed 7,651, up 0.17 percent.

Nasdaq closed 26,523, up 0.4 percent.

The Dow fell 0.18 percent and the Russell fell 0.5 percent.

Technology rose about 0.8 percent. Utilities, materials, and real estate were weak.

Communication services dropped 1.4 percent.

Inside Nasdaq, decliners beat advancers by about 1.42 to 1.

The NYSE was even more lopsided, about 1.78 to 1.

The indexes finished almost flat. The internals were not aligned.

Volume on US exchanges was about 25.3 billion shares, above the 16.2 billion 20-day average.

Triple-witching notional was about 7 trillion dollars, the second-largest on record.

The process can be noisy. The result is often quiet.

Treat this nearly flat close as settlement-week residue. Do not read the sideways tape as risk appetite coming back.

The 10-year yield returned to 5.00 percent from 4.95 percent.

Crude reversed from the 100 area. The Yahoo continuous contract closed 95.47, down 6.3 percent on the day.

Reuters still described inflation pressure from a settlement above 100. The two prints differ by contract month.

For equities, the cleaner tape is that rates are back at 5 percent and commodities are no longer a one-way decline.

The VIX closed 14.81, down 4 percent. The noise was in positioning, not in direction.

The week makes it clearer.

Versus September 11, SPX was almost unchanged, down 0.08 percent. Nasdaq gained 0.7 percent.

The Dow lost 1.7 percent, its worst week since March. The Russell lost 1.5 percent.

QQQ rose 0.9 percent on the week. SOXX rose 1.1 percent.

The weekly tape kept semiconductors and dropped the Dow and small caps.

1 · MARKET

The 520 box opened. 655 is still far

The real move was in chips.

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 2.8 percent, with about 25 of 30 members higher.

SOXX opened 523, high 533, low 522, and closed 533, up 2.7 percent.

It was already above 520 at the open and never returned to the 510 to 520 box.

Thursday had stalled near the trendline. Friday opened the range.

The three-month high is still around 656. 533 versus 656 is leaving the old box, not reclaiming the prior high.

Equipment ran harder than design.

Lam Research rose 7.0 percent. Applied Materials rose 6.5 percent. KLA rose 4.7 percent.

Micron rose 3.9 percent. Broadcom rose 3.0 percent. ASML rose 3.1 percent. Arm rose 4.0 percent.

Nvidia closed 222, up 1.3 percent, high 223 and low 218. That is a little wider than Thursday’s 217 to 220 seam, still not an expansion in volume.

Intel was almost flat. Qualcomm fell 5.8 percent.

Software and communication services did not follow. The chip tape is not uniform.

On Thursday Jensen Huang told reporters that next year’s chip unit shipments should be roughly twice this year’s.

That is units, not a doubling of revenue. The company also does not disclose total unit volume.

Using that remark as a catalyst for Friday’s chip bid is fair. The timing matches, and the semiconductor index was clearly stronger than the broader tape.

Breadth was still negative, so it does not upgrade into a broad risk-on session.

Equipment’s lead is more likely tied to fab expansion and tight tools.

Pulling demand visibility forward fits this green candle. It is still the same narrative, not a new order print.

For the index to rise, chips at least have to hold. That map has not changed.

The five largest companies still account for about 27 percent of S&P 500 next-twelve-months earnings, and semiconductors plus equipment still deliver the bulk of 2027 growth.

SOXX has left 520. The margin for error did not get thicker.

655 remains the cycle high to watch. The path is still hard to draw.

Opening the box only says Thursday’s green bar was not a fake. It does not say the trend has flipped into a run at the old high.

2 · MARKET

QQQ is still 724. IWM is no help

QQQ closed 721, high 722, low 715. 724 still did not give.

Only through 724 does momentum get a look at the 734 area.

Today’s high sat on resistance. The close is still inside 702 to 724.

Settlement volume can pad a step higher, or it can press trapped supply again.

The setup is still neutral. Do not mark 721 as a breakout.

IWM is weaker. It closed 284, high 285, low 283.

288 is already broken, and there was not even an intraday retest.

The three-month head from June is still in place.

Small caps, with a heavy financial and fintech mix, cannot help the tape for now.

301 is still a gap. If IWM gets there, the other indexes are likely already near their highs. Russell is the laggard.

A flat index and strong chips look like rotation. Breadth does not support that read.

Nasdaq had 1,973 advancers and 2,810 decliners.

The bid was semiconductors and a handful of tech names. The longer list went down.

XLC fell 1.4 percent. Netflix fell 4.7 percent.

Writing Friday as a broad tech repair does not match the tape.

3 · SEMIS

AMD keeps leading. Meta repairs first

AMD is still the cleanest structure in the semiconductor complex.

It opened 547, high 560, low 542, and closed 560, up 2.7 percent.

The low stayed above 528. Thursday’s opening discipline did not break.

The three-month high is about 585. Spot is 560, roughly 25 points from that high.

It is stronger than Nvidia.

Whether the semiconductor index can finish the move after opening the box depends first on whether AMD can make the 585 leg stick.

Lose 528, and this uptrend has to be redrawn.

Meta followed the repair map written Thursday.

It opened 689, tagged 690 — the three-month high — then sold to 661 and closed 666, down 2.4 percent.

The low sat in the 656 to 680 pullback band. 656 did not break.

638 is still the split between repair and a downtrend, and that test is not due yet.

After an overbought run, one repair path is to extend first and then fade.

Friday printed both: a tag of 690, then a return into the band.

If the name is still in play, look inside 656 to 680. Do not chase the first print at an overbought high like 690.

Lose 656, and the strong-repair label gets downgraded. Lose 638, and the trend is broken.

Google closed 350, up 0.6 percent, after an intraday 359 that faded.

Amazon rose 1.0 percent.

Microsoft fell 0.8 percent and is still grinding near resistance.

Tesla fell 0.5 percent. 331 and 315 remain the lower map, not the current print.

Most other names have no new structure, so do less.

Triple witching is over. Sensitivity after the positioning reset may be higher than on expiry day itself.

Unless chips hold 533 as the new floor and QQQ clears 724, do not mark Friday as a trend change.
