Rates, Oil, and Earnings

The next week turns on one question. Can Q Q Q hold six hundred ninety eight? On Friday, October twenty third, the Dow fell one point two percent. A I Funding and Data Center Execution

OpenAI is negotiating a new funding round of about thirty billion dollars. The target valuation is one point two five trillion dollars. An earlier target was about one point four trillion dollars. That is a reduction of roughly eleven percent.

The funding round has not closed. The target is still part of negotiations. The debate is shifting toward A I revenue, spending needs, and cash conversion. Compute demand can grow while investors question whether returns will keep pace with investment.

Amazon reached an agreement with Loudoun County to abandon a data center plan on the former George Washington University campus. Most of the site, which covers one hundred twenty two acres, will become housing and mixed use development. Amazon also agreed to donate twenty five acres for community facilities and five million dollars to the county housing fund.

Permits, access to power, and local opposition can delay individual projects. This is a project execution risk. It does not establish an AWS wide investment cut. Watch the company's capital spending guidance, cloud growth, and free cash flow.

2 · SETUP

Positioning Is Tight, but Shorts Have Not Won

Goldman Sachs client positioning data show net de risking in seven of the past eight sessions. The October twenty second reduction ranked as the fifth largest daily cut of the year. Global long short net leverage has fallen to a one year low. Short leverage is close to a five year high.

That setup can extend a selloff if bad news continues. It can also fuel a sharp rebound if the news improves. Positioning alone does not pick the winner.

3 · MARKET

S P Y and Q Q Q Levels

S P Y closed near seven hundred sixty dollars. Seven hundred fifty nine dollars is the near term support level. A confirmed close below it would break the first layer of support. Q Q Q closed near seven hundred four dollars. Six hundred ninety eight dollars is the key downside level. Seven hundred twenty eight dollars is overhead.

A close below six hundred ninety eight dollars that fails to recover would raise the downside risk. Holding that level and reclaiming seven hundred twenty eight dollars would weaken the short term bearish case. Wait for the closing price, volume, and market breadth to confirm. An intraday wick is not enough.

4 · EARNINGS

Wait for Earnings Clarity

Microsoft, Google, and Tesla are all sensitive to upcoming earnings. Wait for guidance on revenue, A I capital spending, and cash conversion. Then compare the stock reaction with the reported results. A clear reversal on rising volume is a stronger signal than a low volume bounce before earnings. There is no need to guess the direction early.
