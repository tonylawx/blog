Market View: The Rally Is Spreading Beyond Tech

U.S. stocks rotated on October twentieth. The Dow gained one point zero two percent. The S and P five hundred rose zero point two eight percent. The Nasdaq slipped zero point zero two percent. The Russell two thousand added about one point seven percent.

Healthcare led the market, while technology weighed on the Nasdaq. Money moved beyond large technology stocks and into healthcare, industrials, financials, and defensive groups.

Market breadth improved, but semiconductors and large technology stocks did not join the move. The artificial intelligence trade has not regained a clear direction. The next test is whether more stocks keep participating and whether technology attracts sustained buying after earnings.

SpaceX: Strong Results, but Discount the Long Term Target

SpaceX reported third quarter revenue of about fourteen point six billion dollars, up one hundred sixty one percent from a year earlier. Net income reached four point four billion dollars, and adjusted E B I T D A was eight point eight billion dollars.

Management discussed reaching five hundred billion to one trillion dollars in annual recurring revenue by the end of twenty twenty seven. That target is far above the current revenue run rate and assumes very high utilization, pricing, computing capacity, and demand. It can lift sentiment, but it should not be treated as a base case.

A potential combination of SpaceX and Tesla also came up, but there is no formal transaction structure to value. Without exchange terms or cash consideration, prediction market odds cannot substitute for a deal proposal.

I S R G and N F L X: Beating Estimates Does Not Guarantee a Rally

Intuitive Surgical generated three point zero five billion dollars in third quarter revenue, up twenty two percent year over year. Adjusted earnings per share reached three dollars. Da Vinci procedure volume grew about fourteen percent, and the company placed five hundred forty six systems.

The company also recorded a four hundred fifty million dollar antitrust settlement charge. The after tax impact reduced generally accepted accounting principles earnings per share by about ninety nine cents. Investors have to weigh the charge, the growth outlook, and the valuation together.

Netflix reported about twelve point eight seven billion dollars in quarterly revenue and adjusted earnings per share of eighty seven cents. Its fourth quarter revenue outlook came in slightly below expectations, keeping attention on viewing time, content strategy, and slower growth. A plan to build a position below seventy seven dollars still needs price stabilization. A post earnings drop alone is not a bottom signal.
