The Selloff Reaches the Next Support Range

On November twentieth, the S&P 500 closed at six thousand nine hundred ten point three nine. That was down three point seven percent for the day and eight point six one percent for the week. The Nasdaq Composite fell five point two eight percent, the Nasdaq one hundred lost five point six one percent, and the Dow declined one point nine three percent, and the Russell two thousand fell two point seven six percent. The volatility index reached forty point zero three before ending at thirty five point eight nine. The market repriced risk sharply. Those readings cannot say whether selling has ended.

The exchange traded fund tracking the S&P 500 closed at six hundred eighty nine dollars and eighty one cents. That places it inside the six hundred seventy one to six hundred ninety four dollar observation range. If subsequent trading confirms a sustained break below six hundred seventy one, the next downside area to watch is six hundred forty eight to six hundred sixty seven dollars. If buyers stabilize the market, the area from seven hundred twenty eight to seven hundred fifty three is a recovery zone to monitor, not an automatic target.

The sixty one point eight percent retracement of the rise from the April low to the October high is about six hundred ninety seven dollars and twenty six cents. Six hundred seventy one dollars serves as a separate scenario level.

The fund's two hundred day average was about seven hundred thirty dollars and twenty nine cents on a dividend adjusted basis, and seven hundred thirty two dollars and seventy six cents on an unadjusted basis. They use different price histories, so the chart and reference should use the same basis.

The Nasdaq one hundred fund closed at six hundred eight dollars and forty five cents, still above the five hundred eighty five dollar reference. Short covering could help a rebound, but it cannot guarantee one. The semiconductor fund S O X X closed at one hundred twenty three dollars and forty cents, down ten point five five percent. Its historical price series must reflect its three for one share split effective November fifth. The fund is different from the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index.

Nvidia Breaks Below a Watched Level

Nvidia closed at one hundred twenty five dollars and fifty three cents, down fourteen point zero one percent and below the one hundred thirty two dollar reference. At about two thirteen in the afternoon Eastern time, Reuters reported that securities regulators were examining potential accounting issues, citing two people briefed on the matter. The agency declined to comment and Nvidia had not immediately responded at the time of the report.

The scope was not disclosed, and an inquiry is not a finding of wrongdoing. Nvidia's shares were already down more than six percent before the Reuters report, so the full day's decline cannot be attributed to it. Investors still need to distinguish confirmed company disclosures from allegations.

Separately, Nikkei reported that at least two of Nvidia's server partners had suspended shipments to customers in Southeast Asia amid changes to U.S. export controls. The report concerns those partners, not a company announcement that all shipments have stopped. The size, duration and financial effect remain uncertain.

A short term bounce would not by itself resolve the reported inquiry or restore the previous chart structure. The investigation report also does not establish how much future revenue will be affected. Watch the companies' disclosures and whether buyers can defend a new range.

Apple Explores Agents as Spending Comes Into Focus

Apple is developing an A I agent to help users operate devices, use apps and browse, Bloomberg reported on November nineteenth. Apple's job listings provide evidence of development work. They do not establish a launch date, final features or commercial success. Google is also investing in A I tools as competition increases.

Apple's fiscal twenty twenty six cash payments for property and equipment were nine point zero four one billion dollars, down from twelve point seven one five billion the year before. That is companywide capital spending, not an A I only budget. Research and development expense was about forty five point eight billion dollars. Apple also uses outside cloud services and leases infrastructure, so lower reported capital spending does not mean its A I work costs nothing.

Analyst Ming Chi Kuo estimated, based on supply chain research, that planned twenty twenty seven shipments of a Google chip called T P U eight I, designed with Broadcom, could be reduced by roughly one million units or about twenty percent. Google and Broadcom have not confirmed that estimate. It concerns that chip generation, not all T P U orders or total A I capital spending.

The investment question is whether useful products can bring in enough revenue to justify computing and infrastructure costs. Hiring, a product announcement and actual customer adoption each answer a different question.

Positioning Can Add Volatility in Both Directions

Trend following funds and options dealers can affect the speed of a move, but models describe scenarios rather than confirmed flows. Seven thousand eighty four on the S&P 500 is a reference in one model for trend following funds. Their trades depend on their positions, rules and subsequent prices. That reference does not prove that buying will begin at an exact level.

November twentieth was a monthly options expiration session. Expiring positions can change hedging needs, but expiration alone does not show the size of all dealer exposures or prove that forced selling is over. Follow through and broader participation matter more than a single move above a model level.

U.S. equity markets have regular sessions Monday through Wednesday next week. They are closed Thursday, November twenty sixth for Thanksgiving and close at one in the afternoon Eastern time on Friday. Thinner participation can make a move harder to interpret.

Taiwan Semiconductor Breaks Below Its Prior Range

Taiwan Semiconductor closed at three hundred eighteen dollars and seventy one cents, down nine point four eight percent. It moved below the previously discussed range of three hundred twenty to three hundred fifty dollars. The next question is whether selling slows and the price can stabilize or reclaim part of that area.

A market commentator described waiting for stabilization before considering a position. That is his conditional approach, not a verified trade or a statement about the listener's holdings. A previously identified range does not guarantee that a falling stock will stop there.

Microsoft, Google, Tesla, Intuitive Surgical, Netflix and Travelers have different business exposures. Their operating results and financing risks deserve separate attention. For the next session, a hold inside the S&P fund's current range could give buyers time to build a rebound. A confirmed move below its lower edge would shift attention to the next downside area. Neither outcome is guaranteed.
