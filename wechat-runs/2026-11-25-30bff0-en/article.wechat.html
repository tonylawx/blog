0 · MARKET

The close looked calmer than the session

U.S. stocks recovered most of their early losses on November twenty fifth. The S and P five hundred finished down zero point three percent. The Nasdaq lost zero point six percent. The Dow gained zero point three percent, and the Russell two thousand added zero point seven percent.

The intraday lows were much worse. S P Y touched six hundred and ninety five dollars and forty five cents. That was about three point two five percent below the previous close. Q Q Q fell as low as six hundred and seven dollars and seventy four cents. That was a four point seven six percent drawdown. S O X X briefly dropped to one hundred and eighteen dollars and eighteen cents. That was more than eight percent below its prior close.

By the close, S O X X had recovered to one hundred and twenty seven dollars and ninety eight cents. It was down only half a percent. S P Y finished at seven hundred and sixteen dollars and seventy cents. Q Q Q closed at six hundred and thirty three dollars and ninety three cents. Both finished less than one percent lower.

Buyers absorbed much of the early selling. The close near the top of the day's range shows demand at the lows. But it does not prove institutions have rebuilt a durable long position.

The V I X did not confirm a return to calm. It reached thirty four point nine five intraday and closed at twenty eight point two nine. That was still above the previous close of twenty six point three six. Equity prices recovered, but the options market continued to charge more for protection.

Eight of eleven S and P sectors finished higher. So the session was not a uniform liquidation. Technology remained one of the weaker groups. Calling this an intraday recovery is fair. Calling it a confirmed market reversal is premature.

1 · MARKET

Three headlines may have helped, but none proves the cause

The F C C decision on SpaceX's Starmind orbital data centre system was real. The order was released on November twenty fifth. It approved part of the application and deferred part of it, with conditions.

It authorizes backup and emergency communications for a proposed constellation of up to one million orbital data centre satellites. The first satellite is not required until twenty thirty three. Later deployment milestones extend to twenty forty. This is a long term infrastructure headline. It is not computing capacity that can serve customers today.

Michael Burry also confirmed that he sold the remaining half of his Nasdaq one hundred put position. In his November twenty fifth post, he said his portfolio was temporarily almost entirely long. He had about twenty five percent in cash. He also said he would keep looking for future short opportunities.

That change could have reduced expectations for more bearish bets. It may also have encouraged some short term traders to take profits. One investor's trade cannot explain the full index move. It does not prove that institutions broadly turned bullish.

Foreign exchange was volatile as well. The Japanese yen strengthened while the U.S. dollar slipped. Dollar yen traded through a wide intraday range. That timing overlapped with the equity rebound. But the timing does not prove that yen carry trade money flowed back into stocks. Yen strength can also reflect safe haven demand or investors cutting leveraged positions.

My read is that short covering and fast money buying likely amplified the rebound. The SpaceX authorization and Burry's update gave traders headlines to trade around. The next test is whether cash equity buyers keep showing up. Volume also needs to confirm the move, and credit spreads need to stop widening.

2 · MACRO

Strong growth, sticky inflation, and resilient jobless claims

October headline P C E inflation rose zero point three percent from the prior month. That was slower than September's zero point five percent. The annual rate edged up from three point seven to three point eight percent.

Core P C E rose zero point three percent on the month. Its annual rate moved from three point one to three point three percent. Monthly inflation cooled a little, but the annual core trend did not. The report gives the Federal Reserve no clean reason to rush toward easier policy.

The second estimate of third quarter G D P was revised up to four point three percent annualized. The advance estimate had been three point four percent. The Bureau of Economic Analysis attributed the revision mainly to stronger investment and consumer spending.

The official G D P tables do not identify sixty five percent of growth as coming from A I investment. A I infrastructure spending matters, but that exact sixty five percent figure is not an official statistic.

The labour market also remained resilient. Initial jobless claims fell to one hundred and eighty nine thousand in the week ended November twenty first. That was below the market expectation of two hundred thousand. Continuing claims declined to one million six hundred and ninety seven thousand.

Markets were still pricing roughly a ninety eight percent chance of a twenty five basis point Fed rate hike in December, according to Reuters. Strong growth supports revenue expectations. Sticky inflation and firm employment also make a quick policy pivot harder to price in.

3 · FOCUS

Nvidia's A A rating is on negative watch

On November twenty fifth, S and P Global Ratings placed Nvidia's issuer and senior unsecured debt ratings on CreditWatch Negative. The ratings remain double A. This is not a downgrade.

S and P said it could cut the rating by one notch within ninety days. That depends on whether concerns about board independence, management oversight, and key person risk are addressed. A larger move would require evidence of damage to Nvidia's reputation or business outlook.

The bigger read through is Nvidia's role in the financing chain. On its third quarter fiscal twenty twenty seven earnings call, Nvidia disclosed one hundred and eighteen billion dollars of total guarantees at quarter end. That included one hundred and five billion dollars of credit support for land, power, and data centre shell capacity. The site will host Nvidia infrastructure under a twenty year lease to OpenAI.

Nvidia also disclosed thirty two billion dollars of neocloud commitments. Data centre lease and cloud service commitments were a little over one hundred billion dollars. These are different categories. They should not be added together. They are also not cash debt due immediately.

A CreditWatch action does not automatically change the ratings of Nvidia's customers. It does not make existing guarantees payable. But lenders may reassess collateral, loan spreads, and future guarantees. Cash rich hyperscalers have more room to absorb those changes. Smaller compute operators that rely on refinancing have less room to manoeuvre.

Credit markets have not offered the same relief as equities. Reuters reported record or near record credit protection costs for several A I linked issuers. These included Oracle, Alphabet, SpaceX, and CoreWeave. Separate coverage also reported fresh highs in credit risk measures for Nvidia and CoreWeave.

Stocks can recover quickly. Multi year financing risk takes longer to reprice.

4 · MARKET

December needs confirmation, not a Christmas rally prediction

The V I X closed at twenty eight point two nine. That is still a high volatility reading. Short covering may have reduced immediate selling pressure. Seasonal buying from E T Fs and long term households could add support in early December. But seasonality is not a guarantee.

For S P Y, the key breakout level in this scenario is seven hundred and fifty three dollars. At the November twenty fifth close of seven hundred and sixteen dollars and seventy cents, that level is still about five percent higher.

Until S P Y clears seven hundred and fifty three dollars on convincing volume, a range bound approach makes more sense. A fast, volume backed break above that level in the first week of December would strengthen the case for a sustained year end rally.

Q Q Q closed at six hundred and thirty three dollars and ninety three cents. The six hundred and thirty three to six hundred and thirty six dollar area is the immediate pivot zone. The five hundred and eighty five dollar area is a deeper support reference. Seven hundred dollars is the next major overhead level.

Q Q Q has higher beta than S P Y. Watch for volume and confirmation from large technology leaders. Do not rely on the index level alone.

5 · MOMENTUM

S O X X has room to rebound, but the repair is not finished

S O X X reached a three month intraday high of one hundred and ninety eight dollars and eighty one cents on October second. It closed at one hundred and twenty seven dollars and ninety eight cents on November twenty fifth. That leaves it about thirty five point six percent below the high.

The intraday low of one hundred and eighteen dollars and eighteen cents attracted buyers. But a rebound from the lows does not repair the medium term trend.

The first rebound zone is one hundred and thirty seven to one hundred and forty four dollars. From the November twenty fifth close, that represents roughly seven to twelve and a half percent upside. A normal round of short covering could help the E T F test that range.

The second target is around one hundred and sixty three dollars. That is about twenty seven percent above the close. It would need more than positioning relief. It would need better fundamentals and sustained buying.

The one hundred and eighty to one hundred and eighty one dollar area is a much higher resistance zone. It is about forty one percent above the close. Only a high volume break and hold above that area would justify revisiting the call that the technical bear market has ended. These are scenario levels, not promised returns.

T S M closed at three hundred and forty two dollars and thirty four cents. It traded as high as three hundred and forty three dollars and seventeen cents. That puts it right at the prior range ceiling around three hundred and forty two to three hundred and forty three dollars.

It touched the level, but still needs follow through. A hold above the range and a successful retest would strengthen the breakout case. A move back below it would weaken the signal. N F L X closed at fifty nine dollars and forty one cents. The near term test is still a volume backed break above its descending trendline, followed by a hold. One rebound session alone is not enough to call a trend change.

My conclusion is that November twenty fifth improved the short term price structure. It may have eased crowded bearish positioning. It did not remove the financing risks in the A I buildout. Watch three things together. Can S P Y clear seven hundred and fifty three dollars on volume? Can S O X X hold its first rebound zone? And will Nvidia related guarantees and neocloud funding costs stop deteriorating?

Price can turn first. Credit risk needs more time to confirm.
