0 · MARKET

A Broad Selloff With an AI Centre

U.S. stocks fell sharply on Monday, November sixteenth. The S and P five hundred lost nearly two percent. The Nasdaq fell about two and a half percent. Semiconductor shares dropped more than five percent.

This is more than a bad day for chip stocks. Investors are questioning how quickly AI spending will turn into cash profits. Some companies with stronger current cash flows are holding up better.

1 · POLICY

Export Rules Reach Overseas Computing

The U.S. Commerce Department has expanded restrictions on advanced chips and remote computing access. A threshold of twelve million total processing performance units applies to certain data centres serving customers in restricted jurisdictions. It is not a blanket ban on every large data centre.

New licensing requirements also affect advanced chip shipments to five countries, including Singapore and Malaysia. The key question is whether customer checks and licence delays slow equipment deliveries.

2 · EARNINGS

Nvidia Faces Its First Earnings Call Without Jensen Huang

Nvidia will report results on Tuesday, November seventeenth, after the U.S. market closes. Colette Kress is now the interim chief executive.

Investors will watch revenue and profit margins. But there is another question. Will Nvidia cut back financing support for some AI cloud customers?

If it does, Nvidia may reduce credit risk. Customers could also find it harder to finance new GPU purchases. That could eventually slow demand. This is a scenario, not an announced policy.

The options market points to a broad event range around one hundred and forty to one hundred and seventy dollars. Those prices are not guaranteed limits.

3 · TSMC

Why Taiwan Semiconductor Is Different

Taiwan Semiconductor has strong advantages in advanced manufacturing. Developing leading-edge processes takes years, and adding capacity is slow. Memory chips generally face sharper commodity cycles.

Even so, no stock has a guaranteed floor. A rough stress scenario values the shares near three hundred dollars, but only under specific assumptions about earnings growth and valuation.

4 · SETUP

What Matters Before Year-End

An options-based reference range for the S and P five hundred runs from roughly seventy-two hundred to seventy-nine hundred points. The upside distance is wider than the downside distance. That does not prove that gains are more likely.

Watch three things. Nvidia's financing commitments. The effect of export controls on actual deliveries. And whether analysts cut the market's earnings outlook for twenty twenty-seven.

A rebound can happen quickly. A lasting recovery needs earnings expectations to hold up.
