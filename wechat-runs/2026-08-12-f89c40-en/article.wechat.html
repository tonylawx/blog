0 · MACRO

Soft CPI, volume edges higher

On August 12 in U.S. Eastern time, the Dow was the only major index lower. The other three finished higher, and volume picked up versus recent sessions. Eight of eleven SPX sectors advanced. Consumer discretionary, materials, and communication services lagged.

July CPI came in broadly in line. Headline rose 0.1% month over month and 3.4% year over year, down from 3.5%. Core rose 0.2% month over month and 2.5% year over year, down from 2.6%. Shelter rose 0.1% month over month and accounted for roughly two-thirds of the monthly gain. Energy fell 1.5% month over month. Auto insurance softened and was a key drag on core. Food rose 0.1% month over month, with food away from home up 0.3%. Medical care, airfares, communication, education, and recreation ran firmer.

The print was soft enough for markets. Money markets lifted the odds of a September Fed hold to about 60%, while still pricing another 25 basis point hike before year-end. Risk assets got relief, but this is not an unconditional cut path.

1 · MARKET

Deleveraging is done; the next phase is re-arming

Institutional leverage data through July 29 show hedge-fund and institutional deleveraging largely complete. Leverage in semiconductors, other tech, and the rest of the book has rolled from the June to July highs toward the March lows.

That reduction in forced selling is what lets SPX hold new highs and the Nasdaq press toward them. Second-quarter earnings were solid overall, and forward EPS still has room to revise higher, so large capital has a plausible window to rebuild leverage. The risk is concentration. If new leverage piles back into a few semiconductor leaders, the market can again print index highs while most names lag. Watch daily volume and key levels to see where the money actually goes.

2 · COMPUTE

Hot cloud and optics names: big upside, expensive defense

CRWV jumped about 19%. The build-out phase still implies expected EPS losses into 2026 and 2027. Take-or-pay style contracts help, but CapEx remains heavy and profitability is not near. The stock is mainly pricing AI growth. Technically, 110 is the first strong resistance after the break of 97.5, with denser supply above. A real path to new highs needs a clear of 143, still more than 30% away. Treat it as a high-volatility, level-driven trade.

NBIS was even sharper. It opened at 226, closed at 259, and gained about 34%. Expected EPS is also still negative into 2026 and 2027. It cleared 231 into a right-side breakout. Overhead between 259 and 267 looks thinner. Trend invalidation sits near 208. Buying near the highs with a stop there implies roughly 20% risk. Fast upside often means fast drawdowns, so size it as a high-beta swing.

LITE rose more than 13% to about 932. On a 30 to 40 times high-growth multiple, fair value around June 2027 is roughly 544 to 726, with a midpoint near 635. Around June 2028, fair value is roughly 868 to 1158, with a midpoint near 1013. Spot is already near the 2028 midpoint, about a year and a half of earnings pulled forward. Cyclical peak earnings are already being marked down, so valuation is only a ruler. After clearing 932, technical resistance above is thin. Swing support is 829. A break back below that would reopen a topping risk.

COHR just reported and trades near 355. At 40 times, June 2027 fair value is about 251 to 335, with a midpoint near 293, so the stock looks a bit rich. In the range, support is 328 to 366 and resistance is 372 to 400. Clearing 400 would reopen a path toward the prior high near 440, where supply is still heavy.

3 · MARKET

Microsoft keeps repairing; AppLovin’s weekly break matters more

Microsoft fell more than 2% on mildly higher volume. Daily RSI overbought and top divergence are still unwinding. Another 1% to 2% down that finishes the overbought reset and a fast/slow line cross would leave a cleaner repair-then-rally path.

AppLovin is the name that matters today. A downgrade questioned roughly 30% growth in 2027 and the stock stayed soft. On a 20 to 30 times multiple the valuation itself is not extreme. Fair value for 2026 is about 315 to 473, and for 2027 about 417 to 626.

The key is the weekly chart. 317, near 310, had been the launchpad since November 2024. A close at 303 confirms the break. Buyers above that zone over the past two years are largely underwater, and the head-and-shoulders is clear. Breaking 317 is a de-risk signal. The next rescue band is 252 to 297. Lose that and the tape can point below 180, another 30% or more from here. Positions bought between 317 and 350 should cut risk once the break is confirmed and keep the loss near 10%, rather than ride toward 252.

4 · SEMIS

Semis vs software: one day does not make a seesaw

SOXX rose more than 2% but opened near 553, stalled in the 550 to 580 band, faded into the close, and sold off into the end of day. Breadth in the components stayed constructive, with 25 up and 5 down.

IGV remains stuck in 101 to 107, closing near 103, down about 0.8%, with software names broadly softer.

One session is not enough to call long semis and short software. Both groups are still pinned under their own resistance. Watch volume and breakouts. If software stays weak while semis truly clear resistance, the seesaw case strengthens. If both break higher in sequence, capital is simply adding on both sides. Who clears first matters more than the slogan.
