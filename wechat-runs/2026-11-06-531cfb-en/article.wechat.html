Payrolls offer no relief on funding costs

United States nonfarm payrolls rose by one hundred and sixty five thousand in October. The two preceding months were revised up by a combined eighty four thousand. Unemployment was four point one percent, while average hourly earnings rose zero point four percent from the previous month and three point one percent from a year earlier.

A sudden labor market slump and immediate policy easing remain poor assumptions on which to base a trade. The number of people working part time for economic reasons also increased. One report offers no blanket all clear for the labor market.

Treasury yields offered no relief for the rebound. In the official daily series, the two year yield rose from Thursday's four point four six percent to four point five one percent. The ten year yield rose from four point eight four percent to four point eight six percent. The curve uses indicative quotes around three thirty in the afternoon, Eastern time, rather than prices at the stock market close.

With short term yields moving higher, long duration construction projects received no obvious break on financing costs. A I computing operators spend before they deliver capacity, so revenue conversion and financing terms remain central to the investment case. A stock price rebound alone is no reason to relax scrutiny of funding risk.

Iren's revenue surges as construction consumes cash

Data center operator Iren closed at twenty one dollars and sixty four cents, up twenty point zero two percent. It almost recovered Thursday's dollar loss, but still ended the week down nine point six five percent. Watch for buying near Friday's low of twenty dollars and eighteen cents, then for a decisive break above the day's high of twenty two dollars and forty six cents.

If a pullback fails to hold, a twenty percent gain does not make the entry attractive by itself. Even if the price stabilizes, delivery and funding milestones still need to come through. Those milestones would provide a stronger basis for confidence in the rebound.

Iren's business is growing quickly. For the first quarter of fiscal twenty twenty seven, which ended on September thirtieth, twenty twenty six, total revenue was two hundred and twenty eight million dollars. A I cloud revenue was two hundred million dollars, up one hundred and eighty two percent from the preceding quarter. That included sixty five million dollars of lease revenue, a category absent in the previous quarter, so the full increase was not like for like growth in service revenue.

Cash generation has yet to catch up with expansion. Iren reported a quarterly net loss under generally accepted accounting principles of two hundred and sixty three million dollars, alongside operating cash flow of five hundred and twenty nine million dollars. The cash flow figure included a positive working capital movement of six hundred and forty five million dollars related to deferred revenue.

That deferred revenue movement cannot all be labeled newly received prepayments. Operating cash flow is also different from operating profit. Two equipment and infrastructure cash payment lines totaled four billion eight hundred and twenty four million dollars, far exceeding operating cash flow. Construction remained dependent on external funding.

3 · MARKET

Some of Iren's nine billion dollars cannot yet be spent

Iren's nine billion dollars represents a combination of funding sources as of the end of September. It includes cash and equivalents, restricted cash, committed financing for graphics processors that has not yet been drawn, and contracted customer prepayments that have not yet been received. Cash and equivalents were three billion five hundred and twenty two million dollars, while restricted cash was two billion two hundred and fifty six million dollars.

That nine billion dollar headline is not a bank balance available to spend at will. Restricted cash releases, financing drawdowns and customer payments each have their own timetable. Some of the money is restricted, and some has yet to arrive.

Iren says funding has been arranged for the zero point three gigawatt program supporting its annualized revenue run rate target of more than four billion dollars. That funding still depends on financing drawdown conditions, escrow release conditions and availability periods. Its remaining capital spending plan for fiscal twenty twenty seven, measured from the end of September twenty twenty six, is approximately fifteen billion dollars.

A separate financing target of approximately six billion dollars supports further expansion and depends on market conditions. That six billion dollar target is for future expansion. It should not be presented as a new funding hole in the existing program supporting the four billion dollar revenue run rate target.

The run rate target has slipped from the end of twenty twenty six to January twenty twenty seven, with a possible further delay to the end of February. It annualizes revenue based on factors including commissioned graphics processors and contract pricing. It is not revenue already recognized under generally accepted accounting principles. Actual revenue may take several more months to ramp after equipment commissioning, testing and customer acceptance.

Watch for completed acceptance, receipt of contracted prepayments and actual financing drawdowns. If construction payments remain ahead of delivery and collections, even a large funding package will not automatically eliminate the cash pressure in between.

4 · MARKET

Demand for older graphics processors does not guarantee lease renewals

Bloomberg reported Friday that Microsoft had been reducing some capacity using older graphics processors rented from Oracle over recent months and had chosen not to renew. Oracle said demand remained strong for earlier generation graphics processors. The report concerns the suitability and renewal of some capacity. Its limited scope does not establish widespread A I contract cancellations or a collapse in aggregate A I demand.

Iren's post quarter contracts offer a counterpoint. Prepayments under a new five year contract cover ninety percent of the estimated capital cost of the related graphics processors. A new H one hundred contract was also priced at twice the level of a specific previous agreement.

Older hardware still attracts customers, but those favorable terms belong to particular projects and contracts. They cannot be applied to every graphics processor. Future orders may not secure the same prepayment terms.

A broader index recovery still needs R S P and the sector ETFs to participate, while Iren needs to connect contracts, delivery and usable cash. Until pullbacks hold the key price levels, a large one day gain is no reason to chase the stock.
