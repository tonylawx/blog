The indexes fall, but breadth improves

Mega cap technology stocks remain under pressure, while most stocks are telling a different story. On Thursday, the Nasdaq Composite fell one point three eight percent, and the S&P 500 lost zero point three zero percent. The small cap ETF I W M rose zero point nine seven percent.

Among S&P 500 constituent securities, four hundred and six advanced and ninety seven declined. Technology and communication services were the only two of the eleven sectors to fall. A few heavyweight stocks dragged down the indexes even as most securities rose.

That divergence leaves open a more constructive path. If cyclical stocks and other sectors keep attracting money while technology corrects, the market's advance could broaden. One day of rotation is not enough, though, and losses in the heavyweights can still push the indexes lower.

Watch both whether technology stabilizes and whether the number of advancing stocks holds up. A single down day in the Nasdaq does not describe the whole market. The pressure intensified after Thursday's close. Earnings from Intel and Amazon delivered successive blows to technology sentiment. Friday's closes in S O X X and Q Q Q will offer a more useful signal than one sharp overnight drop.

1 · EARNINGS

Intel's better quarter meets weak guidance

Intel reported third quarter revenue of sixteen billion five hundred and thirteen million dollars, up twenty one percent from a year earlier. Its non GAAP gross margin was forty four point one percent, and operating margin was nineteen point six percent. Adjusted earnings per share were fifty two cents, above the forty cent consensus.

Under generally accepted accounting principles, gross margin was forty three point zero percent, and operating margin was fifteen point three percent. Earnings per share were sixty cents on that basis. Keep the two accounting bases separate when making comparisons.

The businesses also diverged. Client computing revenue was about eight point one billion dollars, down five percent. Data center and A I revenue reached about seven point three billion dollars, up seventy eight percent.

Foundry revenue was about six point seven billion dollars, up fifty eight percent. Its operating loss narrowed from about two billion three hundred and twenty one million dollars to one billion eight hundred and fifty three million dollars. Foundry revenue includes internal transactions that are eliminated in consolidation.

Fourth quarter guidance is the bigger problem for the stock. Revenue is expected to be fifteen billion to sixteen billion dollars. The fifteen point five billion dollar midpoint is below consensus of about seventeen point zero seven billion dollars.

Non GAAP earnings per share guidance of just twenty five cents also missed the forty three cent expectation. Weak personal computer demand, memory costs, and inventory adjustments remain disruptive. For now, strong data center growth cannot offset the shortfall in the coming quarter. Intel closed regular trading at ninety two dollars and seven cents. It finished the standard after hours session around seventy dollars and eighty six cents. Even after that plunge, the valuation still depends on what Intel can earn next.

The sixty two to seventy two dollar range can serve as the first scenario zone to monitor. If that range fails, the next zone is fifty to fifty four dollars. These are valuation and trading assumptions, not proven price support. Until the personal computer outlook and next quarter guidance improve, manage exposure as a swing trade. A trade in the rebound needs an exit condition before entry.

2 · COMPUTE

Amazon's cloud business outpaces retail and advertising

Amazon reported third quarter net sales of about two hundred and one point nine billion dollars. That was up twelve percent, or thirteen percent excluding currency effects. Sales were slightly below expectations of about two hundred and two point three billion dollars.

Prime Day moved from last year's third quarter to this year's second quarter. The company estimates that the timing change reduced growth by about four percentage points. Do not attribute that entire timing effect to deteriorating consumer demand.

Net income was about twenty point seven billion dollars. Earnings per share were one dollar and eighty nine cents, versus expectations of about one dollar and ninety six cents.

The year over year comparison needs to account for roughly nine point five billion dollars of pretax investment gains in the prior year quarter. This quarter had no equivalent contribution of that scale. The profit comparison therefore does not directly measure underlying operating performance.

Amazon Web Services revenue was about forty six billion dollars, up thirty nine point three percent. Annualizing the quarter gives a run rate of about one hundred and eighty four billion dollars. Cloud demand remains strong.

Advertising revenue was about nineteen point six four billion dollars, up roughly eleven percent. That was below expectations of about twenty point eight billion dollars. A shift in advertising budgets to the fourth quarter is one possible explanation, but next quarter's revenue still needs to validate it.

3 · MARKET

Cash burn increases the funding burden

Amazon is putting capital to work faster than the cash is coming back. Cash purchases of property and equipment reached about seventy seven point three billion dollars in the third quarter, up from about fifty four point two billion dollars in the second. The full year capital spending plan of about two hundred and twenty billion dollars was not raised, but the cash pressure remains substantial.

Trailing twelve month free cash flow was negative forty two point four billion dollars at the end of the third quarter. It had been negative seven point six billion dollars at the end of the second quarter, and positive fourteen point eight billion dollars a year earlier. All three figures cover rolling twelve month periods.

The same free cash flow formula starts with operating cash flow, subtracts cash purchases of property and equipment, and adds proceeds from disposals and incentives. On that basis, third quarter free cash flow alone was about negative thirty three billion four hundred and fifty six million dollars.

Cash proceeds from long term borrowing totaled about one hundred and fifteen point one billion dollars in the first three quarters. That included about forty eight point one billion dollars in the third quarter. The carrying value of noncurrent long term debt rose from about sixty five point six billion dollars at the end of last year to about one hundred and seventy three point one billion dollars.

Third quarter interest expense was about one point seven billion dollars, versus about zero point five billion dollars a year earlier. The one hundred and fifteen point one billion dollar figure measures financing cash inflows. The one hundred and seventy three point one billion dollar figure measures the debt balance at the reporting date. Repayments, reclassifications and other adjustments also affect the difference.

The larger the investment, the greater the pressure to generate cash returns. Cloud growth can support long term investment. But the scale of the buildout makes depreciation, financing costs, and cash conversion increasingly relevant to shareholder returns. The after hours selloff lowers the entry price. It does not automatically resolve those pressures. A credible long term buying opportunity depends on sustained cloud growth, narrowing cash burn, and the indexes holding their watch zones on Friday.

4 · CONSUMER

Microsoft debt: assess cash generation and funding costs

Microsoft has priced twenty five billion dollars of bonds in ten tranches. Maturities range from two to forty years, and settlement is expected on November fourth. A long term bond issue alone is insufficient evidence of a cash crisis. Operating cash flow, capital spending, and debt maturities all need to be assessed.

In the first quarter of fiscal twenty twenty seven, Microsoft generated about fifty eight billion dollars of operating cash flow. It spent about thirty nine point four billion dollars on cash purchases of property and equipment, and produced about eighteen point six billion dollars of free cash flow. Management expects free cash flow to remain positive for the full fiscal year. That is a forward looking expectation, subject to future operating results and spending.

Positive free cash flow at the group level does not show that every A I project has earned back its investment. It also does not establish coverage of every use of cash, including dividends and buybacks. Debt issuance can lock in funding and extend maturities. Whether it is attractive depends on the actual coupons, terms, and future returns.

The offering attracted about one hundred and fifteen billion dollars of orders, or four point six times the deal size. That shows strong investor demand. The deal size remains twenty five billion dollars. Microsoft carries a triple A rating from S and P, and a triple A rating from Moody's. Those ratings support its access to funding and assess debt repayment capacity. The stock price can still fluctuate.

The four hundred and ninety to five hundred and thirty four dollar range remains a scenario zone for considering gradual long term entries. Above six hundred dollars is a valuation target assumption. Earnings and cash flow still need to validate those levels.

One debt offering cannot establish a loss of cash generating ability. Strong bond demand also cannot remove the risk around returns on capital spending.

Q Q Q closed regular trading at seven hundred and fifteen dollars and thirty four cents. It finished standard after hours trading around seven hundred and four dollars and thirty one cents. The six hundred and ninety six to six hundred and ninety eight dollar range is the next scenario zone to monitor, and Friday's close still needs to validate it.

The case for easing selling pressure and a recovery requires Q Q Q to hold that area at Friday's close. It also requires S O X X to hold around four hundred and eighty two dollars and eighty cents. A closing break below those areas would weaken the near term outlook. A cheaper long term valuation cannot replace risk control.

Some weekly and month end options expire on Friday. Closing positions after expiration could ease selling pressure, but a V shaped recovery still needs confirmation from price and volume. It is too early to assume that options flows will pull the market back up.

If support holds without a rebound, bulls and bears are still digesting earnings. Further confirmation is needed. The next political calendar event is the midterm election vote on Tuesday, November third. Results may become clear that evening, the following day, or later. Positions with existing downside protection can use Friday's close as a review point. For capital still on the sidelines, first see whether the watch zones hold, then decide how much volatility to accept.
